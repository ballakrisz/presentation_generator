Motivation & Background
  • Recurrent and convolutional models dominate sequence transduction tasks
  • Attention mechanisms improve modeling of long-range dependencies
  • Sequential computation in RNNs limits parallelization and efficiency
  • Transformer replaces recurrence and convolutions with attention
  • Enables faster training and improved translation quality
Transformer Model Architecture
  • Encoder-decoder structure with stacked self-attention and feed-forward layers
  • Dispenses with recurrence and convolutions entirely
  • Enables highly parallel computation
  • Residual connections and layer normalization in each sub-layer

Attention Mechanisms in Transformer
  • Scaled Dot-Product Attention: computes attention weights via scaled dot products
  • Multi-Head Attention: parallel attention layers capture diverse relationships
  • Allows model to attend to information from different subspaces

Advantages of Self-Attention
  • Shorter paths for long-range dependencies than RNNs or CNNs
  • Enables full parallelization during training
  • Computationally efficient for typical sequence lengths
  • Facilitates interpretability via attention visualization

Key Components of the Transformer
  • Encoder: stack of 6 layers, each with multi-head self-attention and feed-forward network
  • Decoder: stack of 6 layers, includes encoder-decoder attention
  • Positional encodings added to input embeddings to capture order
  • Shared embedding and pre-softmax weights
Training Regime
  • Trained on WMT 2014 English-German (4.5M pairs) and English-French (36M pairs)
  • Byte-pair encoding for vocabulary
  • Adam optimizer with custom learning rate schedule
  • Dropout and label smoothing for regularization
  • Base model: 12 hours, Big model: 3.5 days on 8 GPUs
Translation Results & Efficiency
  • Transformer outperforms previous models on BLEU scores
  • Achieves higher quality at a fraction of training cost
  • Base and Big models both surpass prior single and ensemble models
